FAQ

What areas do you cover?

Warren, Morris, and Sussex counties in New Jersey. Every municipality has a page on this site. If you are on the edge of the area, call and we will say yes or no.

How fast can you get here?

Same-day when a truck is open, especially if you call before noon. Next-day is the normal backup.

What will you not take?

Wet paint, chemicals, asbestos, propane tanks, motor oil, and biohazard material.

Do you donate items?

Usable furniture and household goods go to local charities when they will accept them. Electronics go to e-waste recycling.
